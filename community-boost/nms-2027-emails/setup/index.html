Setting up the confirmation emails in HubSpot

Step by step, for whoever sets this up on your team: two email templates, two automated emails, the partner form and two workflows. The emails themselves are on the preview page. If any step looks different in your account, message us and we’ll walk through it with you.

Before you start

  • A HubSpot user who can edit Design Manager files, marketing emails, forms and workflows.
  • The three files:

    You’re in (HTML)We got your note (HTML)Team alert (text)

  • Your answers for the blanks: who sends each email and reads the replies, who gets partner alerts, how soon you reply to partners, and the name and title that sign the partner email.
  • Menu names are from HubSpot’s current navigation. Where a menu moved, the older name is in brackets.
1

Add the two email templates

About 10 minutes

  1. Open Content›Design Manager (older menus: Marketing›Files and Templates›Design Tools).
  2. Click File›New file. Choose HTML + HubL, then Template, and set the template type to Email. Name it NMS 2027 You’re in and create it.
  3. HubSpot starts the new file with a comment block (it begins with <!-- and includes templateType: email). Keep that block. Select everything below it and paste the whole You’re in HTML file.
  4. Click Publish changes.
  5. Do the same for NMS 2027 We got your note with the We got your note file.
  6. In the We got your note template, replace the four orange [CB: …] notes with your answers (search the file for [CB:). Or send us the answers and we’ll send the file back filled in.
  7. Optional: upload the four pictures to your File Manager and swap their addresses in both templates. Until then they load from our server.
2

Create the two automated emails

About 10 minutes

  1. Go to Marketing›Email and click Create email. Choose Automated, then pick NMS 2027 You’re in from your custom templates.
  2. Fill in the email’s settings: internal name, from name, from address (and reply-to), and the subscription type it sends under (the one summit sign-ups agree to, for example event or summit updates).
  3. Subject line: You’re in, then insert the First name token. Preview text: Your Zoom link, both days in your calendar, and a link for a coworker.
  4. Click Review and publish, then Publish. A workflow can only send an automated email once it’s published.
  5. Repeat with NMS 2027 We got your note. Subject line: We got your note, then the First name token. Preview text: Our team will reply with the 2027 partner options.
3

Create the partner form

About 5 minutes

  1. Go to Marketing›Forms and click Create form. Choose Embedded form and start from a blank template.
  2. Name it NMS 2027 partner inquiry.
  3. Add these fields, in this order: First name, Last name, Email, Company name, Message. These are HubSpot’s built-in properties, and the form on the sponsors page already uses the same ones (firstname, lastname, email, company, message).
  4. Leave the form’s own email notifications off. The team alert comes from the workflow in step 4, so nobody gets two.
  5. Publish the form, then open its Share or Embed code. Copy the portalId and formId values and send them to us. We connect the sponsors page to the form through HubSpot’s Forms API: the page keeps its design, and every inquiry lands in HubSpot as a contact.
4

Build the partner workflow

About 10 minutes

  1. Go to Automation›Workflows (older menus: Automations›Workflows) and click Create workflow, then From scratch. Choose Contact-based and a blank workflow. Name it NMS 2027 partner inquiry.
  2. Set up the trigger: When an event occurs›Form submission, and choose NMS 2027 partner inquiry. Turn on re-enrollment for this trigger, so a partner who writes twice is answered twice.
  3. Add an action: Send marketing email, and choose NMS 2027 We got your note.
  4. Add an action: Send internal email notification. Recipients: the people who handle partners. Subject: New partner inquiry: then insert the Company name token.
  5. Body: paste the alert text below. Then replace each {{ … }} with the matching contact token from the token button (First name, Last name, Email, Company name, Message).
    NEW PARTNER INQUIRY · NMS 2027
{{ contact.company }} wants to talk about partnering.
Someone just sent the partner form on the summit’s partners page.

Name: {{ contact.firstname }} {{ contact.lastname }}
Work email: {{ contact.email }}
Company: {{ contact.company }}
Their note: {{ contact.message }}
Sent from: The partners page form

Reply to {{ contact.firstname }}: mailto:{{ contact.email }}?subject=NMS%202027%20partner%20options

They already got the automatic “We got your note” email, which says our team will reply with the 2027 partner options and what each costs, [CB: the reply time that email promises].

Sent by HubSpot when the partner form on the summit’s partners page is sent.
  6. Review the workflow and turn it on.
5

Build the sign-up workflow (leave it off)

About 5 minutes

  1. Create another contact-based workflow from scratch, named NMS 2027 sign-up confirmation.
  2. Trigger: When an event occurs›Form submission, and choose the summit sign-up form. Turn on re-enrollment.
  3. Add an action: Send marketing email, and choose NMS 2027 You’re in.
  4. Save it, but leave it off. The You’re in email carries each person’s own join link, which comes from the event platform’s HubSpot connection. We’ll send our platform recommendation first. Once you’ve picked one, the step that registers each person on the platform goes before the email, we map the join link, and then it can go live.
6

Test before anything goes live

About 10 minutes

  1. In each email, use Preview as a specific contact, then Send test email to yourself. Open it in Gmail, Outlook and on a phone.
  2. Once we’ve connected the sponsors page, send its form once with a test name. Check that the contact appears in HubSpot, the We got your note email arrives, and the team alert arrives.
  3. Check the subject lines for a contact with no first name.
  4. Tell us when it’s done, and we’ll test the live page against it from our side.
