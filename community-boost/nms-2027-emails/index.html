Setting them up in HubSpot

Everything happens in your HubSpot account. Here’s the short version; the step-by-step guide has every click, the text to copy and the files.

Open the step-by-step setup guide

  1. Load the three emails

    • In Marketing > Files and Templates > Design Tools, create a coded email template for each email and paste in our HTML. The footer already has HubSpot’s required unsubscribe link and company address.
    • Create an automated email from each template. Set the from name, from address, reply-to and the subscription type it sends under (for example, summit or event updates).
    • Optional: upload the pictures (two headers, Cameron’s photo, the calendar icon) to your File Manager and swap their links. Today they load from our server.

  2. The sign-up email (You’re in)

    • A contact-based workflow. Trigger: a submission of the summit sign-up form. Turn re-enrollment on, so someone who signs up again gets the email again.
    • Action: send the You’re in email.
    • Turn it on. The email has no join link in it: that comes in a later email before the summit, once the event platform is set.

  3. The partner form and its two emails

    • Create a HubSpot form, for example “NMS 2027 partner inquiry”, with First name, Last name, Email, Company and Message. These are HubSpot’s built-in properties, and the sponsors page form already uses the same names.
    • Send us your portal ID (Hub ID) and the form’s ID (both are in the form’s embed code). We connect the sponsors page to it through HubSpot’s Forms API, so the page keeps its design and every inquiry lands in HubSpot as a contact.
    • A workflow. Trigger: a submission of that form. Actions: send the “We got your note” email, then a Send internal email notification with our alert’s subject and text to the people who handle partners. HubSpot sends internal alerts in its own plain layout, so the alert above shows the content to paste.

  4. Test before anything goes live

    • Send each email to a test contact from HubSpot (not the template preview) and open it in Gmail, Outlook and on a phone.
    • Send the sponsors page form once: check the contact, the thank-you email and the team alert.
    • Check that a contact with no first name still gets a clean subject line.
