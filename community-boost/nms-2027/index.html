The day your 2027 plan stops being a draft.

It’s the five-part blueprint our strategists write with clients. On Day 1 you draw your own, part by part, with them in the room.

  1. Goals: what 2027 has to raise

    Raise $480K online, up from $312K, and add 400 monthly donors.

    10:30am, the guided hour with Cameron

  2. Audiences: who you’re talking to

    Neighbors who give monthly, weekend volunteers, and local grocers.

    10:30am, the guided hour with Cameron

  3. Channels: where they find you

    Google search through the Ad Grant, the monthly email, and Instagram.

    11:30am, in the Foundation or Scale room

  4. Data: what you check every month

    Online gifts and new monthly donors, plus how much of the Ad Grant was used.

    12:30pm, analytics and reporting

  5. First 90 days: what ships first

    The Ad Grant rebuilt on Day 2, a one-page donation checkout, and the spring appeal on April 14.

    2pm, from Blueprint to Build

A note from your host

Why we’re bringing the summit back.

Community Boost has helped nonprofits reach the people who support their work since 2012: 1,500+ nonprofit partners and $150M+ raised online. We’ve run this summit ten times since 2020.

This year it has a new shape. One day to plan where your marketing goes in 2027, and one day to build it with our team beside you.

“We want you to leave with less stress and fewer things to do. Clarity, and work that’s already done.”

Cameron RipleyFounder and CEO, Community Boost
Fair question

So, what’s the catch?

There isn’t one to worry about. Both days are free.

Community Boost runs this summit because it’s how a lot of nonprofits meet our team. Will we mention what we do? Yes, briefly. You can book a free call with our team, and at the launch party we show our new AI tool.

Neither is needed to take part. Come for the two days, take the work home, and that’s it.

Is it really free?

Yes. Both days are free. Community Boost hosts the summit because it’s how a lot of nonprofits meet our team. At the launch party we show our new AI tool, and there is nothing to buy to take part.

What time is it for me?

Both days run 9am to 2:30pm Pacific, which is 12pm to 5:30pm Eastern.

Which stage is right for me?

Starting or growing your marketing: the Foundation stage. Already have a plan you want to grow: the Scale stage. Everyone meets on the Community stage, and you can move between stages any time.

Do I need to know how to use AI?

No. Day 1 is about your strategy. On Day 2 the team walks you through the tool, and the Support Room is open all day.

Can my team come?

Yes. Send them this page so each person gets their own pass and reminders.
