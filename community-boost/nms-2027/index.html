Questions

Before you register

Anything else? Email summit@communityboost.org.

Is it really free?

Yes. Both days are free, and so are the recordings.

What time is it where I am?

Both days run 9:00 AM to 2:30 PM Pacific, which is 12:00 to 5:30 PM Eastern.

Which stage is for me?

If you're starting or growing your marketing, the Foundation Stage. If you already have a plan and want to scale it, the Scale Stage. Everyone meets on the Community Stage, and you can switch any time.

Do I need to know how to use AI?

No. Day 1 is about your strategy. On Day 2 the Community Boost team walks you through the tool, and the Support Room is open all day if you get stuck.

Can my team come?

Yes. Send them this page so each person gets their own link and reminders.

Will you sell something?

Community Boost will show its new AI tool on Day 2, and you can try it. You don't need to buy anything to take part in either day.
