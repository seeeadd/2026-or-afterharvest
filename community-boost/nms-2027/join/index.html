Is it really free?

Yes. Both days are free. Community Boost hosts the summit because it’s how a lot of nonprofits meet our team. At the launch party we show our new AI tool, and there is nothing to buy to take part.

What time is it for me?

Both days run 9am to 2:30pm Pacific, which is 12pm to 5:30pm Eastern.

Which stage is right for me?

Starting or growing your marketing: the Foundation stage. Already have a plan you want to grow: the Scale stage. Everyone meets on the Community stage, and you can move between stages any time.

Do I need to know how to use AI?

No. Day 1 is about your strategy. On Day 2 the team walks you through the tool, and the Support Room is open all day.

Can my team come?

Yes. Send them this page so each person gets their own pass and reminders.
