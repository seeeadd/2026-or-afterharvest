Q1What can a partner do at the summit?
Take the title partnership, or run a session or workshop for the nonprofit teams who attend. Send the form and our team will reply with the options.
Q2Who runs it?
Community Boost, which has helped nonprofits reach the people who support their work since 2012: 1,500+ nonprofit partners and $150M+ raised online. The 2027 summit is the 11th since 2020.
Q3What does partnering cost?
It depends on how you take part. Send the form and our team will reply with the options and what each costs.
Q4Is the summit free for nonprofits?
Yes. Both days are free to attend, live on Zoom.
