Quick answers

Before the summit.

Is it really free?

Yes. Both days are free, and there's no card to enter. Community Boost has hosted the summit for nonprofit teams since 2020.

Which stage is mine?

The Community Stage is for all nonprofit professionals. Choose Foundation to build a cost-effective marketing strategy or Scale to improve a strategy you already use.

Do I need tech skills?

No. Day 1 focuses on planning. On Day 2, choose a workshop that fits your experience and the campaign you want to work on.

Can my whole team come?

Yes, and it helps. Each person signs up with their own name and email, so everyone gets their own join link. Use your link in step 4.

Do I get CFRE credit?

Community Boost plans to submit sessions for CFRE continuing education credit. Session eligibility and credit hours are pending. We’ll share the details after approval.
