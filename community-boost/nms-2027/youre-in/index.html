Quick answers

Before February 23.

Is it really free?

Yes. Both days are free, and there's no card to enter. Community Boost has hosted the summit for nonprofit teams since 2020.

Which stage is mine?

The Community stage is for everyone. Foundation is for teams building their first real marketing plan, often small and on a tight budget. Scale is for teams with a plan who want it to work harder.

Do I need tech skills?

No. Day 1 is planning. On Day 2 our strategists build alongside you, step by step, in your own accounts.

Can my whole team come?

Yes, and it helps. Each person signs up with their own name and email, so everyone gets their own join link. Use your link in step 4.

Do I get CFRE credit?

Yes, CFRE continuing education credit is offered for attending. We'll send the details in February.
