About

What is New Media Summit?

Media moves fast. Algorithms change, channels stop working, new opportunities are everywhere, and AI is transforming everything.

New Media Summit is a place to step away from your day-to-day grind and join an open and knowledgeable community to discuss the future of media and education.

You'll join 600+ of the world's smartest founders to learn what's new, what's working, what's not, and how to cut through the noise and focus on what matters most.

New Media Summit is 2 full days of actionable content and useful networking. In fact, we have a strict "no theory" policy when it comes to our sessions. Everything you'll see and hear is tested and proven to ensure you're getting what you came for: a plan that will make a positive impact on your business.

If you're a founder, creator, journalist, or marketer, New Media Summit is your event. Welcome home.

We do things differently.

  • No pitches.
  • No doom and gloom or "rah-rah" motivational speeches.
