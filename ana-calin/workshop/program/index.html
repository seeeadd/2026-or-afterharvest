I spent five years not asking.

Here's what not asking costs. Another year of good posts and $0 at the end of every month. Readers who would have paid you, paying someone else instead.

What changed it for me was one decision, made in writing: I put a price on something and told the readers I already had.

The Exit Lab is that decision, taught the way I wish someone had taught me: 30 days, one launch, in a room instead of alone. You don't need five years. You need December.
