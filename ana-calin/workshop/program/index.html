07Why I built this

I spent fifteen years in corporate marketing. One week I crossed three continents, landed in Singapore and fainted on my hotel bathroom floor. Not long after, I walked away, and it took me five years to earn my first dollars on my own.

For a long time the newsletter made almost nothing. I wrote good essays and I didn't ask. What changed wasn't my writing. I put a price on something and said so, in writing, to the readers I already had.

That's still the whole business: $1.4M from the newsletter, and I've never taken a sales call. The three days got you to the start line. This is where it goes out.
