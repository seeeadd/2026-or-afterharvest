Why I built this

I didn't start with an offer. I started with five years of not asking.

I spent fifteen years in corporate marketing. One week I crossed three continents, landed in Singapore and fainted on my hotel bathroom floor. Not long after, I walked away.

It took me five years to earn my first dollars on my own. For a long time the newsletter made almost nothing. I wrote good essays and I didn't ask.

What changed wasn't my writing. I put a price on something and said so, in writing, to the readers I already had. That's still the whole business, and I've never taken a sales call.

This is that system, taught the way I wish someone had taught me. 30 days, one launch, in a room instead of alone.
