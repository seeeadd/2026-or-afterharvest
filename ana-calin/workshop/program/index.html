The core method

Three decisions, made in writing.

Most first offers start with a product, a platform and a price. That can get you started, but it doesn't give your readers a clear reason to buy.

So we start somewhere else: the one reader you write to, the one sentence she'd repeat, and the one ask that tells her where to get it. Everything else is built around those three.

I didn't start with an offer. I started with five years of not asking.

I spent fifteen years in corporate marketing. One week I crossed three continents, landed in Singapore and fainted on my hotel bathroom floor. Not long after, I walked away.

It took me five years to earn my first dollars on my own. For a long time the newsletter made almost nothing. I wrote good essays and I didn't ask. I told myself I'd charge when I was ready.

What changed wasn't my writing. I put a price on something and said so, in writing, to the readers I already had.

The ask didn't change my writing. It changed what my writing was for.
