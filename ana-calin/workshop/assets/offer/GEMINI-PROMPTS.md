# Ana's offer pages: Gemini prompts (hands only, green screens)

Six photos that show where the 30 days fit into an ordinary evening. Same method as the confirmation-page timeline:
no faces, only hands and the device. Every screen is **solid green**, and the example screen from the page goes in
afterwards, matched to its angle, so the text is sharp and right. Until a photo arrives, the pages show the screens
drawn in HTML (they act out what they show: the replies arrive, the test payment goes through).

## How to run them

1. Make photo 1 first. For photos 2 to 6, attach photo 1 and start with: *"Same hands, same ring and sleeve as the
   attached photo."*
2. Landscape, 4:3, the largest size.
3. If Gemini puts anything on the screen (an app, text, a glow), reply: *"Make the screen a flat, even #00FF00 green
   with nothing on it and no reflections"* and regenerate.
4. If it looks staged, reply: *"Less polished. Messier, more ordinary, like a quick photo on a phone."*
5. Drop the finished photos in the chat with their number. I put the screens in and swap them onto the pages.

## Add this to the end of every prompt (photo 6 leaves out the last sentence about the screen)

> Candid photo taken on an iPhone, first-person point of view. Only a woman's hands and forearms are in frame, no face.
> Her hands are in their early 40s, real skin texture, short natural nails, one simple gold ring, the cuff of a soft
> oatmeal knit sleeve. Ordinary indoor evening light, a little uneven, a little grain, slightly imperfect framing, not
> centred, not staged. Real everyday clutter at the edges (a charger cable, a hair tie, a child's crayon, a used mug).
> Not a stock photo, not editorial, no studio lighting, no bokeh balls. No logos, no text, no watermarks. **The device
> screen is filled edge to edge with flat, solid, bright green (#00FF00), nothing on it, no reflections, fully visible
> and not covered by a finger.**
