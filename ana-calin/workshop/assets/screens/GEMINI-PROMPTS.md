# Ana: Gemini prompts for the timeline photos (final, v2: hands only)

Four photos, one per timeline card. No faces: just her hands and the device, shot like she took it on her own
phone. In each, the screen is **solid green**; I put the real screen in afterwards, matched to its angle, so the
text is sharp and right. The Day 2 sheet photo waits until the VIP deliverables are final.

## How to run them

1. Make photo 1 first. For photos 2 to 4, attach photo 1 and start with: *"Same hands, same ring and sleeve as
   the attached photo."*
2. Landscape, 4:3, the largest size.
3. If Gemini puts anything on the screen (an app, text, a glow), reply: *"Make the screen a flat, even #00FF00
   green with nothing on it and no reflections"* and regenerate.
4. If it still looks staged, reply: *"Less polished. Messier, more ordinary, like a quick photo on a phone."*

## Add this to the end of every prompt

> Candid photo taken on an iPhone, first-person point of view. Only a woman's hands and forearms are in frame, no
> face. Her hands are in their mid-40s, real skin texture, short natural nails, one simple gold ring, the cuff of a
> soft grey knit sleeve. Ordinary indoor light, slightly uneven, a little grain, slightly imperfect framing, not
> centred, not staged. Real everyday clutter at the edges (a charger cable, a hair tie, a used mug, a few papers).
> Not a stock photo, not editorial, no studio lighting, no perfect symmetry, no bokeh balls. **The device screen is
> filled edge to edge with flat, solid, bright green (#00FF00), with nothing on it and no reflections. The whole
> screen is visible and not covered by a finger.** No logos, no text, no watermarks.
