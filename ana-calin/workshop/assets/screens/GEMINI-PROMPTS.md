# Ana: Gemini prompts for the timeline photos

Four photos. Each one shows a device with a **solid green screen**. I put the real screen in afterwards, matched to
its angle, so the text is sharp and correct (Gemini garbles screen text). The Day 2 sheet photo waits until the VIP
deliverables are final.

**How to run them**
1. Make photo 1 first. Then, for photos 2, 3 and 4, attach photo 1 and start with: *"Same woman as in the attached
   photo, same look and styling."*
2. Ask for the largest size Gemini offers, landscape 4:3.
3. If the green screen has a glare or a reflection, say *"make the screen a flat, even #00FF00 with no reflections"*
   and regenerate.
4. Send me the photos. I place the screens and put them in the timeline cards on both confirmation pages.

---

## The look, for every photo (paste this at the end of each prompt)

> Photorealistic lifestyle photograph, 4:3 landscape, shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm lens, natural window
> light, soft warm tones, shallow depth of field, editorial magazine feel, real home, lived-in but tidy. Colour
> touches: cream, warm wood, a little soft pink, and sunflower yellow (a yellow mug, or a few sunflowers in a small
> vase). The woman is in her mid-40s, natural and warm, light everyday makeup, relaxed clothes (a soft knit or a
> linen shirt), not a model pose. **The device screen is filled edge to edge with flat, solid, bright green
> (#00FF00), evenly lit, with no reflections, no glare, no interface and no text. The whole screen is visible: not
> cropped by the frame and not covered by a hand or an object.** No logos anywhere (no Apple logo), no text, no
> watermarks.
