# Ana: Gemini prompts for the timeline photos (final)

Four photos, one per timeline card. In each, the device screen is **solid green**. I put the real screen in
afterwards, matched to the screen's angle, so the text is sharp and right (Gemini garbles screen text). The Day 2
sheet photo waits until the VIP deliverables are final.

## How to run them

1. Make **photo 1** first.
2. For photos 2, 3 and 4, attach photo 1 and start the prompt with:
   *"Same woman as in the attached photo, same face, hair and styling."*
3. Pick landscape, 4:3, the largest size Gemini offers.
4. If a screen has glare, a reflection or anything on it, reply: *"Make the screen a flat, even #00FF00 green with no
   reflections and nothing on it"* and regenerate.
5. Send me the four photos. I put the screens in and add them to the timeline cards on both confirmation pages.

## Add this to the end of every prompt

> Photorealistic lifestyle photograph, 4:3 landscape, full-frame camera, 35mm lens, natural window light, soft warm
> tones, shallow depth of field, editorial magazine feel, a real lived-in but tidy home. Colour touches: cream, warm
> wood, a little soft pink, and sunflower yellow (a yellow mug, or a few sunflowers in a small vase). The woman is in
> her mid-40s, warm and natural, light everyday makeup, relaxed clothes (a soft knit or a linen shirt), not a model
> pose. **The device screen is filled edge to edge with flat, solid, bright green (#00FF00), evenly lit, with no
> reflections, no glare, no interface and no text. The whole screen is visible: not cropped by the frame and not
> covered by a hand or an object.** No logos anywhere (no Apple logo), no text, no watermarks.
